QFEC is a unified, highly configurable forward-error-correction decoder designed for communication systems whose standards and channel conditions change over time. A shared architecture supports QC-LDPC, Polar, Turbo, and Convolutional codes while allowing throughput and coding-gain tradeoffs to be configured for both established standards and proprietary systems. Fabricated in a 12 nm FinFET process, the chip reaches 9.97 Gb/s for the Optical Communication Terminal standard and 6.52 Gb/s for 5G BG1, with normalized energy efficiencies of 1.04 pJ/b and 1.53 pJ/b, respectively.
