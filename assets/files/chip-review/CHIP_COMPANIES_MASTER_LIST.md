## 11) AI chip involvement categorization

### A) **Direct AI chip makers** (training/inference accelerators are core products)
- Google, Amazon AWS, Meta, Microsoft, Alibaba, Baidu
- NVIDIA, AMD, Intel, Qualcomm, Broadcom, Marvell, MediaTek, Huawei/HiSilicon
- Cerebras, Groq, SambaNova, Tenstorrent, d-Matrix, Etched, Taalas, Graphcore, Mythic, Hailo, SiMa.ai, Recogni, Rain AI, Untether AI, FuriosaAI, Axelera AI, Rebellions, Positron AI, Tachyum
- Cambricon, Biren, Moore Threads, Enflame, Iluvatar CoreX
- Tesla (Dojo/FSD), Mobileye, Horizon Robotics, Black Sesame, Ambarella

### B) **AI-capable chip makers** (make chips used in AI, but AI accelerators are not their only or primary business)
- Apple, Samsung, Xiaomi, OPPO/Vivo (mobile NPUs/AI blocks)
- NXP, STMicroelectronics, Renesas, Infineon, Analog Devices, Microchip, onsemi, Synaptics, Novatek, Realtek, Ceva, GreenWaves, Ambiq
- Micron, SK hynix, Samsung Memory, Kioxia, Western Digital/SanDisk (memory/storage critical for AI systems)
- Astera Labs, Cornelis Networks, Ayar Labs, Enfabrica, Lightmatter (interconnect/networking/optical infrastructure for AI clusters)

### C) **Not direct AI chip makers (but critical AI chip ecosystem)**
- Foundries: TSMC, GlobalFoundries, UMC, SMIC, Tower
- Equipment/EDA/IP: ASML, Applied Materials, Lam Research, KLA, Tokyo Electron, Cadence, Synopsys, Siemens EDA, Arm, Imagination

## Notes
- Category A = clearly building chips specifically for AI training/inference.
- Category B = chips are used in AI systems, but company is broader than dedicated AI accelerators.
- Category C = essential for enabling AI chips, but they are not primarily shipping standalone AI accelerators.
- For technical blog sequencing, start with Category A first, then Category B.
